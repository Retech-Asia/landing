Our approach to AI

Two separate questions: how we build, and what goes in your product.

How we build with AI

Our engineers use AI tooling to accelerate implementation. Every line that ships is reviewed and owned by an engineer whose name is on the delivery.

AI assists the build. It does not replace accountability.

AI inside your product

We add AI features when the use case justifies them: the task is measurable, your data is ready, and the operating cost is understood before anyone writes a prompt.

No AI for its own sake. Some products are better without it.

Engagement and cost

Three ways to work with us. Costs quoted per scope, never guessed.

Discovery

A scoped engagement that maps requirements, architecture, and a delivery plan before you commit to a build.

Scoped project

A fixed-scope build with defined phases, acceptance criteria, and a delivery timeline you can hold us to.

Ongoing engineering

A dedicated team shipping increments against a prioritized backlog, month after month.

What drives cost

Scope, the number of platforms, integrations with existing systems, roles and permissions complexity, and third-party costs (infrastructure, licenses, model APIs). We quote against these factors after discovery, so the number reflects your system rather than a rate card.

Questions

Asked before every engagement.

What drives the cost of a project?

Scope first: how many workflows, platforms, and integrations the system needs. Roles and permissions complexity and third-party costs (infrastructure, licenses, model APIs) come next. We quote after discovery, against your actual requirements.

Can you migrate our current system?

Yes. Migration from spreadsheets or legacy software is a standard part of our CRM and business-system work. Data is mapped and rehearsed before cutover, and the legacy system stays available until the new one passes acceptance.

How do we communicate during the project?

A named point of contact, a written status per delivery cycle, and review sessions where you test increments hands-on. Average response time under 24 hours.

What happens after launch?

Handover with documentation, access, and source code. Ongoing engineering is available as a separate engagement, and the system is built so another team could take it over.

When does AI make sense in a product?

When the task is measurable, the data exists and is usable, and the operating cost is acceptable. Document intelligence, classification, and retrieval are common fits. We will tell you when a rules-based solution is the better answer.
