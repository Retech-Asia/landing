Round 5

Aurora Sweep II was called good but the hunt continues. Fusion round: the lit grid and tilt-plane languages rebased onto the aurora palette, plus a third idea. All three are single-file WebGL (fragment shader only), calm left for copy, lazy cursor (lerp 0.028), reduced-motion safe.

Direction L

Aurora Grid

Lit Grid's fine engineering grid catches Aurora Sweep II's drifting bands: cells glow where the light pools, fade in the dark, and the whole lattice breathes with the wash. The cursor drags a soft glow cell-to-cell.

 Direction M · front-runner

Lit Dunes

Slow sand-dune heightfield in tilted perspective under an aurora wash: green, cyan, violet, pink and amber bands coexist on the terrain, topographic contour lines wrap the dunes, ridges catch a rim light. The cursor is a lamp: it raises a mound under itself and pools colored glow on the surface.

Verdict: Jay loves it — the pick. Cursor softened twice on his call; final values: lazy trail lerp 0.010, mound 0.15 wide, lamp 0.50 wide pool, parallax 0.07/0.02. None of the lab layers fold in; the base scene ships as-is.
 Direction M2 · feature lab

Lit Dunes Lab

The same dune scene with five optional layers on independent toggles: a wandering lamp that raises its own mound and glow, a breathing sun on the horizon, directional dune shading (lit slopes and shadowed slip faces), wind streak lines rippling across the sand, and a meteor crossing the sky every ~14 seconds. Check and uncheck each to feel what it adds.

Verdict: none of the layers fold in. Jay kept the base scene and asked only for a softer cursor transition, landed in lit-dunes.html. Lab kept as reference.
 Direction M3 · color lab

Lit Dunes · Color Lab

The final dune scene under five color patterns, each grounded in the company identity: 0 the current aurora as reference, 1 Brand Field (the logo and system palette itself: greens, cyan, violet), 2 Retake Loop (the name Retech = retake, the site's green-to-cyan triple gradient), 3 Mekong Dusk (HCMC at dusk: brand greens under gold and violet sky), 4 Asia Signal (home green to international azure and violet, for retech.asia). Flip them live in both themes.

Verdict: push aurora first. Aurora ships in the React port as-is; the four alternates stay parked here for a possible later swap.
 Direction N

Warp Loom

Six woven bands of aurora light on a tilted plane, spanning the full depth from horizon to near field. The cursor is a glass lens: it refracts the bands with chromatic split (red/green/blue fringes) and a faint caustic ring. Bands breathe in depth, shimmer in two counter-scrolling layers, and the whole loom slowly swells and recedes.

Round 4

Lessons applied: 3D is expected, the object must relate to the business, richness over simplicity, capability level rather than literal code. Three bets: H's approved window language at brand level, the delivery story as a globe, and the liked Signal Field concept rebuilt in 3D.

Direction I

Capability Deck

One live product core window fed by three floating module chips (CMS, AI copilot, CRM pipeline) on separate depths, with integration streams flowing chip to core. The counter-tilt see-saw from H, raised to brand level: everything we build, one product.

 Direction J

Global Delivery

A crisp wireframe-and-points globe, HCMC marked with a pulsing beacon, delivery arcs with travelling pulses to Singapore, Tokyo, Sydney, London, San Francisco. Floating proof chips (50+ projects, GMT+7 overlap, EN/VI). The outsourcing story as the object itself.

 Direction K

Living Systems

Signal Field's ink fibers rebuilt on a 3D-tilted plane beneath the copy, calm on the left, accelerating and converging on the right into a compact live product window. Module tags (cms, crm, ai copilot) ride the stream. The liked concept, given depth and a destination.
