## 2. Pattern Synthesis — Top IT Consulting Firm Homepage Structure

The consensus structure across 8 firms, in priority order:

| Section | Used by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero** | All 8 | Universal. Hero increasingly includes a secondary CTA or rotating insight cards. |
| **Logo strip / Trust strip** | 7 of 8 | Either "Trusted by Fortune 500" or specific marquee client names. |
| **Services / Capabilities grid** | 7 of 8 | ThoughtWorks is the exception. Usually 4-8 capability cards. |
| **Engagement models** | 5 of 8 | BairesDev, Saigon Tech prominent; EPAM/Globant fold into services. |
| **Stats / Metrics strip** | 6 of 8 | Kyanon, BairesDev, Saigon Tech, CI&T, EPAM, Globant all show numbers. |
| **Case studies / Featured work** | 5 of 8 | Format varies widely (see below). |
| **Why Us** | 6 of 8 | 3-4 differentiator pillars. |
| **Partner logos (tech partners)** | 4 of 8 | Adobe/AWS/Azure/Salesforce — distinct from client logos. |
| **Process / How it works** | 3 of 8 | BairesDev and Toptal explicit; others implicit. |
| **Testimonials** | 3 of 8 | BairesDev, Toptal, Saigon Tech. EPAM/Globant/CI&T skip entirely. |
| **Awards / Press strip** | 3 of 8 | BairesDev (130+ awards), Toptal (Newsweek), Kyanon. |
| **Locations** | 3 of 8 | EPAM, CI&T, Globant — multinational firms only. |
| **Insights / Articles** | 5 of 8 | Top firms (ThoughtWorks, Toptal, CI&T, BairesDev) treat blog as proof of authority. |
| **Contact / Lead form** | 3 of 8 | Globant, CI&T, Saigon Tech put a full form on homepage. |
| **FAQ** | **0 of 8** | **Nobody puts FAQ on the homepage.** Retech does. |

### Case study format patterns (the key question):

| Format | Used by | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **No case studies on homepage** | ThoughtWorks, CI&T | Push to `/clients` subpage. |
| **One featured case study** | EPAM, Globant | Single marquee client story (FIFA, Bacardi). |
| **Inline rotating case study cards** | BairesDev | Mixed with testimonials inside a single trust block. |
| **Multi-card grid (8+ entries)** | Kyanon, Saigon Tech | Vietnam firms — most similar to Retech. |
| **Named-client metrics strip** | EPAM | "16x deployment capacity **for Bacardi**" |

### What NO competitor does:

- **None have a dedicated "products" section on the homepage.** Products (where they exist, e.g. Toptal's coaching product, CI&T FLOW, ThoughtWorks AI/works) are either mentioned inline or linked out. None of the 8 firms devote a homepage section to showcasing in-house products.
- **None have a separate "products showcase" AND "case studies" AND "portfolio" section.** They pick one format and commit.
- **None put FAQ on the homepage.** FAQ always lives on `/faq` or `/contact`.

## 4. Concrete Recommendations for Retech

### Recommendation 1 — Eliminate the products-vs-case-studies ambiguity

Pick one of two strategies the market has validated:

**Option A: "No products on homepage" (recommended — matches 8/8 competitors)**
- Remove `ProductShowcase` and `ProductTabs` from homepage
- Move product showcases to a dedicated `/products` route (or fold into `/case-studies`)
- Keep `SuccessStories` as the single proof-of-work section, but **upgrade it**:
  - Increase from 3 → 5-6 cards (Kyanon shows 8-9)
  - Each card = client descriptor + named deliverable + outcome metric (EPAM pattern: "16x deployment capacity for Bacardi")
  - Add filters by engagement model (Saigon Tech pattern) once you have enough volume

**Option B: "Products + case studies as distinct types" (only if Retech's products are real commercial products, not project work)**
- Keep one product showcase (merge `ProductShowcase` + `ProductTabs` into a single section — never both)
- Define clear semantic difference:
  - **Products** = commercial software Retech sells to multiple customers (e.g. a SaaS dashboard product)
  - **Case studies** = one-off client engagements
- If Retech's "products" are actually bespoke client builds, they are case studies, not products — use Option A.

**Given Retech's actual work (Signsbeat, Dune — bespoke client platforms), Option A is correct.** What Retech calls "products" are case studies in competitor taxonomy.

### Recommendation 2 — Section reordering to match consensus

Current Retech order over-indexes on proof-of-work (3 sections) and under-indexes on engagement models, insights, and process. Recommended new order:

```
1. Hero
2. TrustedBy (logos / marquee clients)
3. Stats bar (restore — short numeric strip: years, projects, engineers)
4. ServicePreview (what we do)
5. EngagementModels (NEW — ODC / Dedicated Team / Staff Aug / Fixed Price)
6. SuccessStories (single, richer proof-of-work section — 5-6 cards)
7. WhyRetech (differentiators)
8. WhyVietnam (competitive advantage — keep, this is Retech-specific)
9. TechStack
10. Process (NEW — 3-4 step "how we work")
11. Testimonials
12. Insights/ Blog teaser (NEW — 3 latest posts, builds authority per ThoughtWorks/CI&T pattern)
13. HomeCTA
```

### Recommendation 3 — Move FAQ off the homepage

Zero competitors put FAQ on the homepage. Move `HomeFAQ` to `/faq` (which already exists). Replace the slot with either an **insights/blog teaser** (3 latest posts) or a **process strip** (3-4 steps).

### Recommendation 4 — Kill the mid-page team-collaboration photo section

The team-collaboration photo between TechStack and SuccessStories is filler. No competitor does this. Either fold the photo into the WhyRetech section as a supporting visual, or remove it entirely. It currently breaks section flow and doesn't add proof.

### Recommendation 5 — Add named-client metrics (EPAM pattern)

EPAM's strongest proof pattern is the metric strip where every number is tagged with a client name: "16x deployment capacity **for Bacardi**". Retech's current SuccessStories are descriptive; each should lead with a metric attributed to a client (even anonymized: "3x faster release cadence for a Singapore fintech").

### Recommendation 6 — Consolidate rather than expand

Retech's homepage at 14 sections is the longest sampled (median competitor: 6-8). The pattern from top-tier firms (ThoughtWorks: 5 sections; EPAM: 6; Globant: 5) is **homepage as routing hub**, not exhaustive pitch. Cut sections, push depth to subpages.

## 5. Summary

**The single most important change:** collapse `ProductShowcase`, `ProductTabs`, and `SuccessStories` into **one** case-studies section with 5-6 well-structured cards. No competitor in the sample shows in-house products on the homepage; Retech's "products" are case studies in market terminology.

**Secondary changes:** move FAQ off homepage, restore a short stats strip, add engagement models, add a process strip, add an insights/blog teaser. Net result: shorter homepage, higher signal density, matches the proven pattern.
